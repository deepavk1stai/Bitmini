Compliance layers

Risk controls, KYC, AML, sanctions, and governance are built as launch gates.

KYC / AML / sanctions

Wallet onboarding includes a policy baseline for KYC review, AML screening, and sanctions checks before a wallet can move into broad live usage. The system is designed to block unsupported jurisdictions, high-risk wallet profiles, and restricted account activity without allowing unrestricted transfer behavior.

Governance and controls

Admin roles are separated from normal wallet actions. Review controls, escalation paths, and wallet risk states are enforced to keep operational decisions accountable and auditable. Sensitive actions require both valid session identity and explicit policy checks.

Launch-readiness model

Bitmini uses a staged, hybrid model: a custodial default for operational support and recovery, self-custody opt-in only where policy and legal review allow it, and a strong default posture of “do not launch live money services without governance sign-off.”

Operational checklist

Before any public wallet launch, legal entity setup, sanctions screening vendor selection, KYB review, payment-rail approval, and incident-response plans must be complete. This prototype intentionally keeps those controls visible as launch gates rather than pretending they are already live.
