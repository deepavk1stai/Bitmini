Beta wallet and internal-ledger prototype

Bitmini gives everyday people a fairer, simpler way to explore a wallet-first digital value system.

This project is an internal-ledger beta and research prototype, not a live regulated wallet product or a real-money exchange. It demonstrates wallet onboarding, supply controls, referral logic, and network tooling in a controlled testing environment.

PoS: stake and validate

Proof of Stake lets users help secure the network by staking coins. It is energy-light, mobile-friendly, and rewards participants for honest validator behavior over time.

DPoS: delegate with ease

Delegated Proof of Stake allows everyday holders to delegate to trusted validators from a phone. It keeps participation simple while preserving transparent validator accountability.

PoA: stable throughput

Proof of Authority is useful for controlled bootstrap phases and reliable network throughput. It helps deliver predictable confirmations during early growth cycles.

What problem Bitmini solves

Millions of people still deal with expensive moving parts, unclear ownership, poor onboarding, and central points of failure. Bitmini was designed to solve that by combining wallet simplicity with transparent network rules, a hybrid custody model, and accessible participation.

  • Expensive transfers and confusing money rails
  • Opaque governance and limited account control
  • Hard-to-understand digital value experiences

Why Bitmini is attractive

It makes digital value feel human: easier to set up, easier to use, easier to trust, and easier to explain. With wallet-first onboarding, transparent supply rules, and strong security checks, it is built to win confidence and adoption.

Mobile wallet handling and earnings

Bitmini is designed so users can register, secure, send, receive, and monitor wallets directly from mobile devices. Earning paths are built into normal usage so growth can happen without technical complexity.

  • Watch-ads rewards with daily wallet limits for anti-spam control
  • Referral payouts for onboarding real users into the network
  • Validation rewards through PoS, DPoS, and PoA participation
  • Simple wallet actions optimized for phone-first use

Progressive referral rewards

Users are not hard-limited to a small monthly invite count. Instead, Bitmini uses a progressive reward curve: invites can continue, while payout multipliers taper as monthly referral volume increases. This keeps growth open and discourages reward abuse.
