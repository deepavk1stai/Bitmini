Launch readiness snapshot

Public launch is gated by governance, compliance, and deployment controls.

Required before launch

  • Legal entity, risk controls, and board sign-off
  • KYC / AML / sanctions provider integration
  • Admin governance and approval workflow
  • Database hardening and backup assurance
  • Incident response and monitoring plan
  • Geographic restrictions and regional compliance review

Product checkpoint

The current project is a controlled beta wallet prototype. It demonstrates a secure architecture, product framing, compliance hooks, and launch controls, but it is not yet ready for unrestricted real-money customer onboarding. The near-term goal is staged launch readiness rather than live public trading.
